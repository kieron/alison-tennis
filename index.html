Common Questions

Tennis Court Maintenance FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we're asked most often.

How much does tennis court resurfacing cost?

Tennis court resurfacing costs vary depending on the size, current condition, and surface type required. Contact Advantage Court Maintenance on 07771 884762 for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your specific requirements.

How often should a tennis court be resurfaced?

Most tennis courts need resurfacing every 4-8 years depending on usage, climate exposure, and maintenance. Regular cleaning and maintenance can extend the life of your surface. We offer maintenance programmes to keep your court in top condition.

Do you work on domestic tennis courts?

Yes, we work on both domestic (private garden) tennis courts and commercial courts for clubs, schools, and councils. No job is too small - we provide the same quality service whether it's a single private court or multiple club courts.

What areas do you cover?

We cover the whole UK from our base near Louth, Lincolnshire. Much of our work is across Lincolnshire — Lincoln, Grimsby, Louth, Boston, Skegness, Horncastle, Market Rasen, Sleaford, Grantham, Spalding and Stamford — plus surrounding counties including Leicestershire, Nottinghamshire, Cambridgeshire, Norfolk and Yorkshire. No location is too far: we're happy to travel anywhere in the country for tennis court maintenance, resurfacing and construction projects.

How long does tennis court resurfacing take?

The duration depends on the scope of work, weather conditions, and surface type. A typical resurfacing project takes several days to complete. We'll provide a clear timeline when we quote for your specific project.
